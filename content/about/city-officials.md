<h2>Mayor &#8211; James Mayer</h2>
<p>James was born in Burnet, Texas&nbsp;and his Spring Branch roots run deep.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;He is the grandson of Ottie &amp; Alfred Jonas&nbsp;, great grandson of Meta &amp; Willie Fischer and great, great grandson&nbsp;of Hans Heinrich Dietrich Knibbe the original founder of Spring Branch.&nbsp;&nbsp;James spent 27 years in the truck leasing business with Ryder, 10 years as a Harley-Davidson dealer in North Texas and&nbsp; returned to the truck leasing business&nbsp;in 2013&nbsp;with Rush Enterprises in New Braunfels.&nbsp; For the&nbsp;past 30 years&nbsp;James has&nbsp;managed multi-million dollar business units, built business plans and led the teams to manage them.&nbsp; All the while identifying problems and implementing solutions to&nbsp; challenges as they&nbsp;arose.&nbsp; Although running unopposed, James is a totally qualified candidate for mayor and offers the following perspective:</p>
<p>&#8220;Preserving Spring Branch for&nbsp;the next and all&nbsp;future generations is the most important reason for incorporating.&#8221;</p>
<h2>Commissioner &#8211; Brad Hockman</h2>
<p>Brad, his wife Sandra and two children have lived in Spring Branch for&nbsp;17 years and&nbsp;have owned&nbsp;a business on Hwy 281 for the last 16 years.&nbsp; Brad was born and raised in Clinton, Iowa and graduated from the University of Iowa in 1992 with Degrees in Economics and Business Administration.&nbsp;&nbsp; His work experience as a vendor and Development Manager for the US Military along with his knowledge of local conditions will be a valuable asset to the community.&nbsp;&nbsp; Brad spends his free time rooting for the Hawkeyes &amp; the Spurs.</p>
<h2>Commissioner &#8211; David Dobbins</h2>
<p>David and his wife Missy moved to Spring Branch in 2016. What was supposed to be a temporary stop while they built in New Braunfels quickly turned into a love for the area and a permanent home. David has over 30 years in the technology industry, most recently leading teams that build large, high-volume software systems for distributed computing. When not in front of a computer screen you might find David exploring the area (or the US) by bicycle, working to foster rescue dogs, or tending to his bees.</p>
