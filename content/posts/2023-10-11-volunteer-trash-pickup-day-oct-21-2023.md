<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We have set a date for a Volunteer Trash Pick Up Event along Spring Branch Road.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Please come out and join me and your neighbors Saturday, October 21<sup>st</sup>&nbsp;from 9 to NOON.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Trash dumpsters, provided courtesy of Hill Country Waste Solutions, will be placed at the intersection of Rittiman and Spring Branch Roads Friday October 20<sup>th</sup>. Reflective Vests provided courtesy of the Bulverde Spring Branch Chamber of Commerce. Trash collection bags provided by the City of Spring Branch.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We’ll hold a brief Safety Orientation and distribute bags by the dumpsters at Rittiman at 9:00 sharp.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">We hope you can make it out help us clean up the Main thoroughfare of our City and beautify your neighborhood. Dumpsters will be on site Friday-Sunday so if you can’t make it Saturday morning, please feel free to make a deposit at your convenience.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Please email us at info@cityofspringbranch.org if you have any questions.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">HUGE thanks to our friends at Hill Country Waste Solutions!</p>
