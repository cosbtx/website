<p>To all residents and property owners in Spring Branch and the surrounding area;</p>
<p>We are all gravely concerned about the potential for the spread of the Corona virus in our Country, our State and Comal County.<br />
While there have been no reported or confirmed cases in Comal County, the consensus is that it will reach our area at some point in the near future.</p>
<p>Our best defense is good common sense which includes limiting your exposure to large gatherings, taking practical precautions like frequent hand washing and use of sanitizer and keeping your hands away from your face.</p>
<p>I am attaching a link to the State Department of Health Services website, where you can find updates regarding the virus as well as current information about the current cases and resources available to you. That link is <a href="https://www.dshs.texas.gov/coronavirus/"><span>www.dshs.texas.gov/coronavirus/</span></a><br />
In addition, here is a link to the County health department for local information and updates: <a href="http://www.comalcountytx.com/health.htm"><span>www.comalcountytx.com/health.htm</span></a></p>
<p>If you have any specific concerns or questions regarding our City, please email me. I will respond as quickly as possible.</p>
<p>Regards,<br />
Mayor Mayer<br />
City of Spring Branch</p>
