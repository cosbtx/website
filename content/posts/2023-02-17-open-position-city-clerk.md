<h3>Public Notice</h3>
<p>The City of Spring Branch is seeking candidates for the position of City Clerk.</p>
<p>This position is a contract position and requires an average of 20 hours per month.</p>
<p>Required skills include finance and accounting, using Excel and Word as well as scanning and posting documents to the city website.</p>
<p>Candidates must be able to attend all monthly and special meetings as they occur and are required to prepare minutes and agendas.</p>
<p>Resumes and questions may be sent to <a href="mailto:info@CityofSpringBranch.org">info@CityofSpringBranch.org</a></p>
