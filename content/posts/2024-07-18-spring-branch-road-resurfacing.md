<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Friends and Neighbors,</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Repairs and re-surfacing of Spring Branch Road will begin on or around July 30. The first phase will be repairs and leveling up of low areas of the road surface, and will take 1-2 weeks to complete. Once that is done the asphalt has to cure for another 1-2 weeks before the chip/seal surface can be laid down. There will activity on and off for most of August.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Notices will be posted of the work status and signs will placed to alert you when and where work is happening. Additionally, there will be flaggers in place to control traffic flow when the major sections are being done.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Please slow down and exercise patience while this work is being done!</p>
