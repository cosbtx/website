<h3>City of Spring Branch</h3>
<p>We have scheduled a Public Town Hall Meeting for the purpose of hearing your comments on future planning and zoning within our City Limits. Additionally, we will hear comments on our proposed tax rate for fiscal year 2018-2019.</p>
<p>Mark your calendars and make plans to join us at Knibbe Ranch on Saturday morning, July 28 from 10AM until noon.</p>
<p>This is intended to be an open forum whereby you can share your concerns and thoughts with your City Officials.</p>
