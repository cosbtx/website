<p><span>All,<br />
This morning Judge Krause signed an executive order requiring the use of face masks by all employees and customers of businesses in Comal County.<br />
This is a measure that is not popular with everyone, but is essential to slowing the spread of Covid-19. I commend the Judge for his action.<br />
To emphasize the severity of our situation, the case count rose by over 100 cases from yesterday, the largest single day jump yet.<br />
Below is a link to the Order, which can be found on the Comal County website.</span></p>
