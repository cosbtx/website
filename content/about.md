The City of Spring Branch is an incorporated general-law municipality in Comal
County, Texas. Founded in 1852 and incorporated in 2015, the city exists to
preserve local self-governance for its residents.

## City Commission

The city is governed by an elected City Commission, which meets in regular
session and conducts the business of the city in open, posted meetings.

## What the city does

Spring Branch maintains the public roads within its jurisdiction and keeps an
open record of its meetings, ordinances, and finances. This website is the
city's official source for that information.
